THE ETERNAL CONSCIOUSNESS

MAHADEV

The One Beyond Time

Shiva is stillness and movement, destruction and creation, silence and the infinite sound of existence. He is Mahadev — the great consciousness that exists beyond beginning and end.

KNOW MAHADEV

Who Is
Lord Shiva?

Lord Shiva is one of the principal deities of Hinduism and is known as Mahadev, the Great God.

He represents transformation, meditation, detachment and the eternal nature of consciousness.

Shiva is often portrayed meditating in the Himalayas, surrounded by silence and nature. His form reminds us that true strength can exist in stillness.
